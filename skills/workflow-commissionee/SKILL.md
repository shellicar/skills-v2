# Workflow commissionee

`workflow` holds what both sides share; this is the commissionee's half.

**`read.mts` is yours, on a conversation your brief pointed you at.** Reaching past that
means commissioning another conversation or watching one, which belongs to whoever
commissioned you and not to the brief you were given. Nothing stops you running any of
it, since a script cannot see who is calling it, so knowing which side you are on is the
whole of it.

An original message commissions something in a conversation that did not ask for it. If
you find you need to send one, that is a thing to report upward, not to send outward.

If you are blocked on something above you, say so in your own conversation and stop:
whoever commissioned you is watching that conversation, so you never have to wait to be
read.
